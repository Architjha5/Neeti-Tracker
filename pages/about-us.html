ABOUT NITI TRACKER

Making Government More Transparent.

Niti Tracker is a digital platform designed to help citizens and public institutions monitor government projects, budgets, progress and accountability in one transparent system.

Technology for Better Governance

Government projects affect everyday life. Niti Tracker brings project information closer to the people so that progress, public spending and concerns can be monitored more clearly.
